We are committed to providing swift, professional, and compassionate emergency care.

To ensure every patient receives safe attention, our clinic operates under a strict priority triage policy:

01. Critical Emergencies First

Life-threatening trauma, severe breathing issues, or collapse are served immediately upon arrival.

02. Senior & Pregnant Clients

Accommodated next to minimize stress and physical discomfort during wait times.

03. Frightened or Anxious Pets

Pets easily triggered by other animals are given private holding rooms away from other animals.

04. Direct Arrival Protocol

Call ahead at (555) 911-PETS so our staff can prepare emergency equipment before you arrive.

Recognizing Critical Symptoms

Seek immediate emergency assistance if you observe any of the following signs in your pet:

  • Severe or laboured breathing and continuous coughing
  • Accidental ingestion of poisons, toxins, or foreign objects
  • Inability to urinate or extreme distress while attempting to urinate
  • Trauma from vehicle accidents, fall injuries, or severe bleeding
  • Seizures lasting longer than 3 minutes or repeated fitting episodes
  • Sudden collapse, paralysis, or unresponsiveness
