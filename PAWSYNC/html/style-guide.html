Spacing and radius

Accessibility checklist

Check every page against this list before it is pushed.

  • Focus outline: Every link, button and field shows a visible outline when you press Tab.
  • Skip link: A "Skip to main content" link is the first thing on the page.
  • Labels: Every field has a visible label, not just a placeholder.
  • Tap targets: Buttons and links are at least 44 × 44 px.
  • Alt text: Every meaningful image has alt text. Decorative images have an empty alt.
  • One H1: Each page has exactly one <h1>.
  • No color-only status: Every status has a text label, such as EMERGENCY or Full.
  • Contrast: Text has at least 4.5:1 contrast.

Do and don't

Do Keep it simple

  • Use one coral button per screen.
  • Use spacing in multiples of 8 px.
  • Write button text in sentence case.

Don't Crowd the screen

  • Don't use two coral buttons on one screen.
  • Don't use odd spacing, such as 5 px or 13 px.
  • Don't write button text in Title Case or ALL CAPS.
